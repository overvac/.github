OVERVAC is a Croatian software development company focused on custom software and
exceptional web experiences. From ambitious websites and cinematic digital
experiences to tailored software solutions, we design and build around what each
client actually needs.

### What we do

**Custom software** — business software, web applications and mobile applications
built around the way a company actually works. From focused tools to full-scale
digital products, designed and developed around your goals and processes rather
than the other way round.

**Web design and development** — websites that bring together strong visual
direction, thoughtful interaction and solid engineering. From focused business
sites to immersive, animated and real-time 3D experiences, everything built
in-house from concept to launch.
